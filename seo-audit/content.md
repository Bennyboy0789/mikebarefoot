## Issues

### Critical
1. **Site-wide: no usable contact route beyond the form (needs Mike).** Email is null, no phone. Event planners expect a direct email/phone and a response-time promise. Ask Mike for the new speaking email and a booking phone or scheduler. /contact then says "Mike replies by email" with no timeframe; add "typically within X business days" (needs Mike).
2. **Site-wide: canonical K word unresolved.** "Knowledge" (Campbell) vs "Kindness" (Toastmasters). This is the signature framework, and a planner or an AI answer engine quoting it could be wrong. Resolve before launch (needs Mike).

### High
3. **/coaching is thin (415 words, service page floor 800).** It has no formats, cadence, duration, process, outcomes or FAQ, and no coaching testimonial. Fixes that only rework existing facts: add a "What a first conversation looks like" section from the Getting-started copy, a "Who Mike has coached" section built from his real roles (recruiter advising 1,000+ placements), and an honest "Pricing and formats shared on a call" line. Needs Mike: formats, length, whether remote, any client outcomes he approves, any coach credential or certification (do not imply one).
4. **/contact is a form-only page (238 words).** Acceptable for a contact page, but add an FAQ for planners from facts that exist: keynote typically 60 minutes, formats (keynote, workshop, panel, virtual via Teams), audience-size options. Needs Mike: travel policy, AV needs, lead time, fee range or "on request", and whether he speaks outside NC.
5. **Evidence for headline stats is missing.** $50M+ revenue, 60+ media features, 15+ years of profitability and 1,000+ placements are quote-worthy but unsourced on-page. Link the USA Today / CareerBuilder / SIA pieces (needs Mike for URLs), and state "per host introduction, Campbell 2026" where that is the source.
6. **Testimonial attribution gaps.** The Wake Tech quote is unnamed ("Former Director of HR"). Confirm the writer will be named, or label it clearly. The pull-quote on /contact is a real, named testimonial (good). Add a date to each testimonial (needs Mike).
7. **Undated engagements.** Four of five speaking engagements have no year. The speaking table reads as vague for planners. Needs Mike: years, audience size, and any additional events (only the Campbell 2026 event is dated).

### Medium
8. **Duplicate and near-duplicate copy.** The CtaBand "Bring the KAIZEN playbook to your stage." repeats on /, /about and /speaking. The "37 years / 27 years" hook appears in descriptions, hero, intro lines and bio on every page. The Campbell basketball camp and Raleigh story is told on / and /about, and the KAIZEN six-letter block is rendered on / and /speaking. Meta descriptions are unique and well-written (good). Vary the CTA copy by page (e.g. /about: "Hear the story live").
9. **Facts needing confirmation before indexing.** The "4th-degree jiu-jitsu and kickboxing" ranks, Hall of Fame years, and "ranks as worded here" are TODO(mike). Any error here is a Trust and YMYL-adjacent credibility risk.
10. **Citation readiness.** Strong, quotable atomic facts exist, but they are in design-heavy cards, not prose. Add a short "Quick facts / Speaker bio" block on /about (50-word and 150-word bio, plain text, copyable) that an AI engine or event program can lift. Only use existing facts. Also add a 2-3 sentence definition of the KAIZEN keynote near the top of /speaking (existing keynoteSummary does this; keep it as prose above the fold).
11. **No FAQ or Q&A copy anywhere.** Add 5-6 planner FAQs, answered only with confirmed facts; mark unknowns "needs Mike".
12. **Structured data is Person only on all 5 pages.** Content-side: add visible facts to back future Event/Service/VideoObject markup (that part is for the technical audit).
13. **Contact page headings.** Only an H1; the sections (what happens next, based in Raleigh) lack H2s.

### Low
14. **Readability.** The copy is short-sentence and conversational (est. Flesch ~60-70, grade 8-9), with good voice. Some fragmentary taglines ("Two worlds. One discipline.", "Six principles. Continuous improvement.") repeat a rhythm on every section; vary them so the style does not read as templated.
15. **Marketing superlatives without proof**: "elite", "elite recruiter", "high-energy", "one of the greatest kickboxers" (third-party fact; fine). Replace "elite" with the concrete awards already listed.
16. **Possible spelling inconsistency**: Lundy-Fetterman vs the sign-off "Lundy Fetterman" (verbatim letter; leave it). RedZone/Red Zone TODO.
17. **Wisdom Journey podcast has no host and date is vague**; add the host (needs Mike).
18. **Image alt text**: several decorative-empty alts on content photos (campbell-keynote-poster, mike-wide, mike-portrait); describe them where they carry meaning. Contact face photo has alt="" next to a caption; fine.
19. **Freshness**: pages lack visible dates. Add "2026 Leadership Summit" context (present) and a "Updated" signal only if blog or news is added. A short "News / Appearances" list with podcast dates (existing) can serve.
